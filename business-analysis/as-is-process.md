# As-Is Process

1. API metrics are collected.
2. Analyst reviews dashboards or spreadsheets.
3. Analyst manually compares metrics with SLA thresholds.
4. Analyst identifies affected APIs.
5. Analyst investigates the related business process.
6. Analyst prepares an incident summary.
7. Stakeholders are notified.
8. Management report is prepared manually.

## Pain Points
- Repetitive comparison work
- Delayed identification
- Inconsistent severity classification
- Technical metrics are not directly mapped to business impact
- Manual reporting
