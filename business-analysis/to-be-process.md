# To-Be Process

1. Performance data is uploaded.
2. SLA rules are applied automatically.
3. Breaches are detected.
4. APIs are mapped to business processes and criticality.
5. Severity is assigned using documented rules.
6. AI generates a business-friendly summary.
7. BA reviews the output.
8. Incident/stakeholder communication is prepared.
9. Management SLA report is generated.

AI is advisory. The BA remains responsible for validation and business decisions.
