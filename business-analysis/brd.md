# Business Requirements Document

## BR-001
The solution shall compare API performance against configured SLA thresholds.

## BR-002
The solution shall identify SLA breaches across availability, response time and error rate.

## BR-003
The solution shall map APIs to business processes and criticality.

## BR-004
The solution shall classify breaches using documented business rules.

## BR-005
The solution shall generate a business-friendly incident summary.

## BR-006
The solution shall provide management-level SLA compliance reporting.

## Stakeholders
- Business Analyst
- Product Owner
- API/Integration Owner
- Operations Manager
- Business Process Owner
